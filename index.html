About me

Hi, I am Siqi Wang, a master's student in Computer Science at the University of Pennsylvania. Before transitioning into computer science, I studied art history at UC Berkeley and the Courtauld Institute of Art. My interdisciplinary background has shaped how I approach technology, with a strong interest in both human-centered design and technical problem-solving.

My current interests include robotics, computer vision, reinforcement learning, natural language processing, and AI agents. I am especially interested in how language, perception, and learning can help robots and autonomous agents reason, make decisions, and interact more effectively with people in open-ended environments.
