About me

Hi, I am Siqi Wang, a master's student in Computer Science at the University of Pennsylvania. Before transitioning into computer science, I studied art history at UC Berkeley and the Courtauld Institute of Art. My interdisciplinary background has shaped how I approach technology, with a strong interest in both human-centered design and technical problem-solving.

My current interests include robotics, computer vision, reinforcement learning, natural language processing, and AI agents. I am especially interested in how language, perception, and learning can help robots and autonomous agents reason, make decisions, and interact more effectively with people in open-ended environments.

Select Projects

  • Clio

    Tech Stack: RAG, Python, Flask, OpenCV, Google Gemini API, MET Museum API, JavaScript, HTML/CSS, gTTS

    Clio is a mobile-friendly web application that identifies artworks through a user’s camera and provides an interactive museum-guide experience. It uses OpenCV with SIFT feature detection and FLANN matching to recognize artworks, retrieves verified metadata from The Metropolitan Museum of Art API, and uses Google Gemini to answer contextual questions about each piece. The application also supports conversational history and text-to-speech responses for a hands-free voice experience.

  • HomeSync

    Tech Stack: Python, Django, PostgreSQL/SQLite, Bootstrap, Chart.js, HTML/CSS, Gmail SMTP, Gunicorn, WhiteNoise

    HomeSync is a full-stack web application that helps household members organize chores, share responsibilities, and track contributions. Users can create or join households through invitation codes, assign and prioritize tasks by urgency, upload photo proof of completion, and communicate through task comments. The platform also includes user profiles, configurable email notifications, time-zone support, and visual analytics for monitoring household participation and completed work.
